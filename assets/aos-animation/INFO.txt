-------------------------
`AOS.js`--> just the scroll trigger library
`Animate.css`--> the effects library(a CSS file with collection of pre-built animation keyframes — fades, slides, bounces, zooms, etc.)
JS in `AOS.js` watches the scroll position and at the right moment applies `Animate.css` classes to elements.
-------------------------->
<section 
    class="selection-block3-section animate__animated animate__slideInLeft"
    data-aos="fade-left"
    data-aos-duration="1500"
    data-aos-delay="300"
    data-aos-offset="80"
> --> with `animate.css` classes and this case `animate.css`library should be include 👉❌ NOT recomended

<section class="selection-block3-section" 
    data-aos="fade-left"
    data-aos-duration="1500"
    data-aos-delay="300"
    data-aos-offset="80"
> --> Use ONLY `AOS` for scroll animations and it's enough✅

1. 
fade (== fadeIn in `WOW.js`)
fade-left|fade-right (== slideInLeft|slideInRight OR fadeInLeft|fadeInRight in `WOW.js`)
fade-up|fade-down (== slideInUp|slideInDown OR fadeInUp|fadeInDown in `WOW.js`)

2. zoom-in|zoom-out|zoom-in-down|zoom-in-up|zoom-in-left|zoom-in-right (== zoomIn|zoomOut|zoomInDown|zoomInUp|zoomInLeft|zoomInRight in`WOW.js`)

3.flip-left|flip-right| flip-up|flip-down

3. No`bounce` equivalents, No`rotate` variants, No`Big variants`,No`hinge`,No`rollIn/rollOut` in AOS
4. Big variants — No available in AOS 

Full AOS effects list — what you actually have:
    fade
    fade-up | fade-down | fade-left | fade-right
    fade-up-right | fade-up-left | fade-down-right | fade-down-left
    zoom-in | zoom-in-up | zoom-in-down | zoom-in-left | zoom-in-right
    zoom-out | zoom-out-up | zoom-out-down | zoom-out-left | zoom-out-right
    slide-up | slide-down | slide-left | slide-right
    flip-left | flip-right | flip-up | flip-down
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`data-aos-duration` - animation speed
`data-aos-offset` - trigger earlier/later on scroll (относительно нижней части окна браузера I think ??).
`data-aos-delay` - wait how many seconds before animation starts

in main.js file must:
AOS.init({ duration:1000, once:true, offset:100 }); //Initialize the `AOS animation library` (!)It's a bundle with 'aos-css'+'animate-css-4'

Good combination to use:
	--> data-aos-duration="1500" data-aos-delay="300" data-aos-offset="80"
	--> data-aos-duration="800"data-aos-delay="100"data-aos-offset="120"
Modern UI sweet spots:
	duration: 400ms – 800ms 
	delay: 100ms – 300ms
	offset: 50px – 120px

________________________⚠️⚠️⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT:
🧠 Best practice (real agency)
👉 Use ONLY `AOS` for scroll animations AND optionally `animate.css` for special effects, BUT NOT both for same element!
